Can you buy?

Eligibility, financing and the DPS

Who can buy

Singapore Citizen families, or two or more singles aged 35+ under the Joint Singles Scheme.

Household income up to S$16,000 a month.

How much you can borrow

The 30% Mortgage Servicing Ratio applies, as well as TDSR.

At the income ceiling, a bank loan tops out around S$1.01M.

Deferred Payment Scheme

If offered: pay a 20% downpayment, the other 80% only at TOP.

Upgraders can stay in their HDB flat while the EC is built.

First-timers may also qualify for CPF Housing Grants. I check your exact position with your HDB Flat Eligibility letter.

Sources: HDB, ERA. DPS carries a higher price than the normal scheme; confirm terms at launch.
